Lahjati

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Lahjati is an app that teaches spoken Gulf Arabic. It is run by Muhammed Koya, Wayanad, Kerala, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Lahjati app and website collect, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the iPhone app, the Android app and the website.

1. What we collect

  • Your email address or phone number, when you create an account. You log in with a one-time code that we send to it.
  • Your name, if you type it, so that it can be printed on your certificate.
  • Your learning data: the dialect you chose, the levels and job packs you finished, your scores, streaks and app settings (for example language, theme and avatar).
  • Purchase records: which dialect you unlocked, when, the price and currency, and a payment or receipt reference. We do not receive or store your card or bank details.
  • The date you started each dialect, because the dialect choice becomes final once you start the classes.

We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

2. Your microphone and voice

The microphone is used only when you tap the record button in a speaking check. Your speech is turned into text by the speech-recognition service of your phone or browser (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android and in Chrome). That service may send the audio to Apple's or Google's servers to recognise it, under their own privacy policies. The app only compares the text with the sentence you were asked to say. We do not record, store or receive your voice.

3. Why we use your data

  • To log you in and keep your progress safe, so you can continue on another phone.
  • To unlock the dialect you paid for and to check that a payment is real.
  • To make your certificate.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

4. Payments

In the iPhone and Android apps, you pay through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles your payment details under their own privacy policies and tells us only that a purchase was made. If we use a service that checks app-store receipts for us, it receives that receipt and an anonymous app user ID only.

5. Who stores or sees your data

  • Supabase: stores your account, progress and purchase records.
  • Netlify: hosts the website and the lesson audio that the app plays or saves for offline use. Like any web host, it keeps short technical logs (such as IP address and time of request).
  • Google Fonts: the website loads its fonts from Google, which receives your IP address when it does so.
  • Apple and Google: app downloads, payments and speech recognition, as described above.

These providers may keep data on servers outside your country. We share data with them only so that the app can work. We may also share data if the law requires it.

6. Data kept only on your phone

If you use the app without an account, your progress is kept only on your phone or in your browser. It is deleted when you reset the app, uninstall it or clear the browser's data.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account data until you delete your account. When you delete it, your account, progress, purchase records and dialect start dates are deleted from our database straight away. Apple and Google keep their own records of your purchases under their policies.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Account → Delete my account. You can also ask us by email. See how to delete your Lahjati account.

9. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us from the address or phone number on your account so that we can confirm it is you. We reply within 30 days.

10. Children

Lahjati is made for adults and older teenagers who want to learn Gulf Arabic. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each learner can reach only their own records. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep access to your email or phone safe.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app.
