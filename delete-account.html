Delete your Lahjati account

You can delete your Lahjati account and its data at any time. Lahjati is run by Muhammed Koya.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Lahjati and log in.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. In the Account box, tap Delete my account.
  4. Tap Yes, delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the app, email koyasmd1@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Lahjati account". Send it from the email address on your account, or write the phone number you log in with. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We delete it within 7 days and reply to tell you it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address or phone number)
  • Your name, learning progress, scores and settings
  • Your unlock (purchase) records and dialect start dates

Nothing is kept by us after deletion. This cannot be undone: the dialects you unlocked are no longer linked to you.

What is not deleted by us

  • Apple and Google keep their own records of app purchases under their own policies.
  • Progress saved only on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app or reset it.

See also the Lahjati Privacy Policy.
